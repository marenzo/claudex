Compact: [c/x] CLAUDEX

The wordmark is plain ASCII. The compact gateway mark preserves the C/X
identity when a four-row wordmark would take space from operational data.
Color is optional: lavender for CLAUDE, mint for X. Preserve the letterforms
when color is unavailable. Never stretch letters or animate the wordmark.
