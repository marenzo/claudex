Compact: [c/x] claudex

The lowercase wordmark is plain ASCII. The compact gateway mark preserves the c/x
identity when a five-row wordmark would take space from operational data.
Color is optional: lavender for claude, mint for x. Preserve the letterforms
when color is unavailable. Never stretch letters or animate the wordmark.
